FAQ

Common questions

What size of company do you work with?

Most clients have between $20M and $500M in revenue — big enough for complex problems, small enough that senior attention makes a real difference.

Do you work remotely or on site?

Both. We typically spend one to three days a week on site during delivery phases and work remotely the rest of the time.

Can fees be linked to results?

Yes. For Partnership engagements we often tie 20–30% of fees to agreed, measurable outcomes.

How quickly can you start?

Diagnostics can usually begin within two weeks of signing. Larger programmes start within four to six weeks.

Not sure where to start?

A two-week Diagnostic is the lowest-risk way to find your biggest opportunities.

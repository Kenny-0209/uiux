About Meridian

Operators first. Advisors second.

We started Meridian because too much consulting ends at the slide deck. Our partners have run plants, sales teams and finance functions — so we measure ourselves by what changes after we leave.

Built for the companies big firms overlook.

Mid-market companies drive a huge share of the economy, yet they're often stuck choosing between global firms built for the Fortune 500 and solo advisors without the bench to deliver. Meridian sits in between: senior, practical and sized for your reality.

Today we're a team of 48 consultants across three offices, serving clients in manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, software and consumer goods. We're independent, partner-owned and proudly unglamorous about getting the work done.

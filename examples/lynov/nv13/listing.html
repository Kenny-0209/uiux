About this home

Set on a quiet, tree-lined crescent a short walk from Northfield Park, this architect-designed home pairs warm timber cladding with floor-to-ceiling glazing. The open-plan ground floor flows from a chef's kitchen to a sunken living room and out onto a south-facing terrace.

Upstairs, four generous bedrooms include a main suite with walk-in wardrobe and spa-style bathroom. At the end of the landscaped garden, a fully insulated studio makes the perfect home office, gym or guest room.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Chat with our team, we usually reply in under 5 minutes.

How long does it take to set up Pulse?

Most teams see their first live dashboard within five minutes. Install the SDK, paste your project key and Pulse starts auto-capturing events immediately.

What counts as an event?

Any tracked action: a page view, a click, a custom event like “Invoice sent”. Auto-captured and custom events count the same and you can filter out noise anytime.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

Yes. Switch whenever you like from your billing settings. Moving to yearly applies the 20% discount instantly and credits any unused monthly time.

Is Pulse GDPR compliant?

Yes. Choose EU or US hosting, enable cookieless tracking, and sign our DPA in one click. PII is masked by default in session data.

What happens when my trial ends?

You drop to the free Starter plan automatically. No surprise charges, and your data and dashboards stay right where you left them.

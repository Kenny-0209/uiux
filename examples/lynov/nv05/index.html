About

I draw first, then design. The pencil keeps things honest.

Before opening any software I fill pages with small, fast sketches. It’s how ideas stay loose long enough to become surprising. The result is work that feels crafted rather than assembled — identities with character, illustrations with a point of view.

I’ve worked in-house at a publishing house, co-founded a risograph studio, and taught editorial illustration at ESAD. Today I work directly with founders and creative teams, usually on two or three projects at a time.

Our story

  1. 2019

    The first hello

    A bookshop in Brooklyn

    Two people reaching for the last copy of the same novel. James let Amelia have it — on the condition she tell him how it ended.

  2. 2020

    First date

    Coffee that became dinner

    A two-hour coffee turned into dinner, then a walk across the bridge at midnight. Neither of us remembers what we ate.

  3. 2022

    Home

    A small apartment & a big dog

    We moved in together and adopted Biscuit, who has opinions about everything, especially the couch.

  4. 2026

    The question

    She said yes (after crying)

    On a hike in the Catskills, at the top of the trail where we first said “I love you,” James got down on one knee.

Good to know

Questions & answers

Can I bring a plus-one?

We've included plus-ones on invitations where we can. Your RSVP will show the number of seats reserved for you.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones! Children are welcome at the ceremony and dinner; a sitter-staffed kids' room will be open during the dancing.

What happens if it rains?

The ceremony moves into the Glasshouse — same time, same magic, less mud.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, free parking is available on site. If you plan to celebrate, please take the shuttle or a taxi home.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We're having an unplugged ceremony — please keep phones away until we've said “I do.” Snap away at the reception!

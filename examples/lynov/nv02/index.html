FAQ

Good questions

Is Sprout really free?

Yes. Tracking unlimited habits, reminders and streaks is free forever. Sprout Plus ($3.99/month) adds focus soundscapes, health sync and advanced insights.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 16 or later, Android 10 or later, plus companion apps for popular smartwatches.

Can I import habits from another app?

Yes. Import from a CSV export in Settings → Import, and Sprout will map your history and streaks.

What happens to my data?

Your data is yours. Journal entries are end-to-end encrypted, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

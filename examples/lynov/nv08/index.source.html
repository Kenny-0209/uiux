// FAQ

Good questions.

Will talks be recorded?

Yes. Every talk on all three tracks is recorded and sent to ticket holders within two weeks. Workshops are not recorded.

Can I transfer my ticket?

Tickets are fully transferable until 5 November — just update the attendee name from your confirmation email.

What's your refund policy?

Full refunds until 12 October, 50% until 29 October. After that, transfer your ticket to a colleague instead.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes, and we enforce it. Our safety team is reachable on site and by phone throughout both days.

What's included for food?

Breakfast pastries, a hot lunch with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options, unlimited coffee and the day-one riverside party.

Do you offer invoices for companies?

Yes — add your company name and VAT number at checkout and we'll issue a VAT invoice automatically.

Don't miss the lineup drop.

Get speaker announcements and ticket deadlines. About two emails a month.

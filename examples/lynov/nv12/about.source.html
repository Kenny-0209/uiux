About

I photograph what it feels like to be there.

I grew up between the fjords and the North Sea, where the weather changes six times before lunch. That taught me patience — and that the best light usually arrives just as everyone else goes home.

For fourteen years I’ve worked for travel magazines, outdoor brands and couples who want their day remembered honestly. I shoot with small, quiet cameras and natural light, and I spend as long editing as I do shooting.

Work with me

How I work

  1. Listen

    A call to understand the story, the place and the people. No shot lists unless you want one.

  2. Scout

    I visit locations at the time we’ll shoot, so I know where the light falls before the day.

  3. Shoot

    Relaxed, unhurried sessions. I direct gently and then get out of the way.

  4. Deliver

    A hand-edited online gallery within three weeks, plus archival prints if you’d like them.
